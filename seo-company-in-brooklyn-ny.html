What do you need from me to get started?

What is the value of SEO?
SEO compounds. Every dollar spent on paid ads stops working the moment you stop paying, but rankings you earn through SEO keep bringing in traffic long after the work is done. Instead of renting visibility one click at a time, you're building an asset that gets more valuable as it climbs.
When should I expect leads or sales?
